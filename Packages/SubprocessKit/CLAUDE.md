# SubprocessKit

Loaded when working under `Packages/SubprocessKit`. Plan and rollout notes: `docs/SUBPROCESS_KIT_PLAN.md`.

- **What it is** — the one place that spawns a child process: `posix_spawn` plus pipes, no `Foundation.Process`, zero dependencies and no dependency on `Penumbra`. `SubprocessRunner.run(_:onOutput:)` (async: `Task` cancellation, timeout, live chunks) and `runBlocking(_:)` (sync callers) take a `SubprocessRequest` and return a `SubprocessResult`. A non-zero exit, a signal, a timeout and a cancellation are results; only a failed start throws `SubprocessError`. Users: `SystemProcessRunner` and `SystemGradleProcessLauncher` (JavaIntelligence), `SystemGitRunner` (GitIntelligence), Umbra's `AgentCommandRunner`, AgentEval's `CheckRunner`. `Penumbra`, `EditorIntelligence` and `AgentKit` do not link it. Not covered: `JavaDebugProcessLauncher` (bidirectional pipes), SwiftTerm's PTY.
- **A request** — `executable` (absolute; `argv[0]` is that path), `arguments`, `workingDirectory`, `environment` (`nil` inherits), `standardInput` (`.closed` = `/dev/null` by default, or `.data`), `output` (`.separateStreams` or `.merged`), `stdoutCapture` / `stderrCapture` (`.all`, `.bounded(head:tail:)`, `.discard`; what is dropped is counted in `CapturedOutput.omittedBytes`), `processGroup` / `killGroupOnExit`, `timeout` and `terminationGrace` (SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after it; zero is SIGKILL at once).
- **Rules that must stay true**
  - Always `POSIX_SPAWN_CLOEXEC_DEFAULT`, and signal dispositions and the signal mask are reset in the child: an ignored `SIGTERM` or a blocked mask inherited from the app made children unkillable except by SIGKILL.
  - Blocking never runs on the Swift-concurrency pool. Pipes are non-blocking descriptors on a `DispatchSourceRead` on the job's private serial queue; the child is reaped on one dedicated `Thread` (`waitid(WNOWAIT)` first, so its pid cannot be reused while a signal might still be sent, then `waitpid`). `runBlocking` is the same job plus a semaphore, never a `Task`.
  - Both output pipes are always drained together. A result does not wait for EOF: after the leader is reaped, what is in the pipes is taken and the run ends, so a grandchild that keeps the pipe open cannot hang it.
  - Writing stdin sets `F_SETNOSIGPIPE`; a child that stops reading ends the write quietly instead of killing the app with SIGPIPE.
  - `posix_spawn_file_actions_addchdir_np` (the plain name needs a newer SDK than macOS 14).
  - Gradle's launcher signals the client only (`processGroup: false`, 5 s grace): its daemon is shared, and a group kill would end it. The agent and AgentEval use a group plus `killGroupOnExit`.
- **Tests** — `swift test --package-path Packages/SubprocessKit` (also with `--sanitize=thread`; the package is lock- and descriptor-heavy). `SpawnTests`, `PipeTests` (both streams past 64 KB, stdin larger than a pipe, a child that exits without reading stdin), `TerminationTests` (timeout, cancel, group, grace, leader-only), `OutputCaptureTests`, `UTF8ChunkDecoderTests`.
